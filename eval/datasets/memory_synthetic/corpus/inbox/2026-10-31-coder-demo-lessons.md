# coder: lessons from the demo pack

- Scanned invoices below 200 dpi lose the VAT number on about one page in five; ask clients for 300 dpi.
- The table extractor confuses the "Qty" and "Unit" columns when their header cells are merged; a header-alias list fixed it.
- The demo pack runs in 4 minutes on the workstation for 40 invoices.
